Format › Alignment has Align Left (Ctrl+L), Center (Ctrl+E), Align Right (Ctrl+R), and Justify (Ctrl+J). The same four are buttons in the action bar.

## Whole paragraphs

Alignment belongs to a paragraph, so it applies to every paragraph the selection touches, even when only a few words are selected. The current one is checked.

- **Left** and **Right** line text up on that margin and leave the other edge ragged.
- **Center** centres each line between the indents.
- **Justify** spreads the words so that both edges are straight; the last line of the paragraph stays as it is.

## Style or direct

A style sets a default alignment; choosing another here is direct formatting on that paragraph only. To go back, use [Normal](clear-direct-formatting) or Revert to style in [Paragraph Properties](paragraph-properties). To set it for a whole kind of paragraph, edit the [style](modify-style).
