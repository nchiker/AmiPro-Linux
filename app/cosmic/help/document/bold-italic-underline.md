Bold, Italic, Underline, and Word Underline are toggles in the Format menu, with buttons in the action bar. Bold is Ctrl+B, Italic Ctrl+I, and Underline Ctrl+U. Word Underline has no shortcut.

## Selected text and the next text

With text selected, the attribute is switched on for the selection, or off if the whole selection already has it. With only the caret showing, the choice applies to what you type next: press Ctrl+B, type, press Ctrl+B again, and you get a bold stretch inside plain text. The choice lapses if you move the caret before typing.

## What the check mark shows

The check mark shows what you see. A heading whose style is bold shows Bold checked, and choosing Bold there turns the bold off for that text. At a caret with nothing selected, an attribute is shown when the character on either side of it has the attribute.

## Underline and Word Underline

**Underline** draws the line under spaces as well as letters. **Word Underline** leaves the spaces bare, so only the words are lined.

These are direct formatting, laid on top of the paragraph's style. [Normal](clear-direct-formatting) takes them off again. To make every paragraph of a kind bold, change its [style](modify-style).
