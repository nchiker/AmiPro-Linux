Format › Bullets and Numbering › Bullet and Number Style… opens Style Properties for the caret's paragraph style with only the Bullets and numbers section unfolded. The text right-click menu has it too.

## The section

- **Number** with a format: 1, 2, 3; I, II, III; i, ii, iii; A, B, C; or a, b, c.
- **Bullet** with a choice of bullet shape.
- **Text** is typed before the number or bullet; typing spaces first pushes the item in.
- **Right align** ends the bullet or number a space before the paragraph's first word.
- **Attributes** (bold, italic, underline, word underline, all caps, superscript) apply to the bullet or number alone.
- **Space for** is the same value as the style's First indent, the room the bullet sits in.
- **Outline level** and its reset options belong to multi-level numbering; see [Outline styles](outline-styles).

## One bullet in the text

Insert › Bullet… opens a grid of seventeen bullets in rows of six; each has a name as a tooltip. Insert puts it over the selection or at the caret. The bullet behaves as one character, takes the colour of the text around it, and is remembered for next time. Copied to the clipboard, it becomes the nearest ordinary Unicode character.
