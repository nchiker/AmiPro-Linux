**View › Show Clean Screen** (F11) makes the window full screen and hides the menu bar, toolbar, tabs, and status bar, so only the pages remain. It is for reading or writing without distraction.

A small **Return** button stays in a corner. Press it, or press Esc, to bring everything back. Keyboard shortcuts keep working while the chrome is hidden.
