Format › Normal (Ctrl+M) returns the selected text to what its style says.

## What it removes

- Character formatting laid on the text directly: bold, italic, underline, caps, special effects, and a face, size, or colour chosen with Font….
- The alignment, indents, and spacing set on the selected paragraphs, so a centred paragraph goes back to the alignment of its style.

A paragraph's own ruler (tabs placed on it) stays; remove that with [Remove Ruler](rulers).

## What it does not touch

The paragraph's style, its page layout, and frames are not changed. If a heading still looks wrong after Normal, the style itself holds the setting: see [Modify Style](modify-style).

Undo brings the formatting back; one Normal is one undo step.
