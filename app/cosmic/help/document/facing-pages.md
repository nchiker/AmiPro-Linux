These two View items choose how pages sit in the workspace.

- **Continuous Pages** stacks the pages in one column.
- **Facing Pages** shows them in pairs, left and right, like an open book. Page 1 stands alone on the right, then even pages sit on the left with the odd page after them on the right.

Each tab remembers its own choice. The arrangement only changes the view; page layouts and printing are not affected. Zoom out (see [Views and zoom](view-zoom)) to fit a spread on a small window.
