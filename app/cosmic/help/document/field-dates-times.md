Insert › Date/Time… opens the Fields panel at its Date and time page.

## Choices

- **Today's date (as text)** puts in plain text that never changes.
- **System date (updates)** and **System time (updates)** are fields that show the date or time whenever they are brought up to date.
- **Date of last revision** shows when the document was last saved.
- **Date created** shows when it was made.

**Format** offers 21 samples of the date, such as a numeric form or one with the month in words. The panel shows the result before you press Insert. The format list for times has nine forms.

## Changing one

Put the caret in a date field and open Edit › Edit Field… (or double-click it). The *This field* section lets you choose another format. See [Updating fields](field-updating) for when a date is refreshed.
