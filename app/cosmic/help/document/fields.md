A **field** is a place in the text whose content is worked out: the date, the page number, a figure number, or a value from the document's properties. The programmable ones are called **power fields**.

## Inserting and editing

- **Insert › Field…** opens the Fields panel in the rail at its Power field page ([Power fields](power-fields)).
- **Insert › Date/Time…** puts in the date or time ([Dates and times](field-dates-times)).
- **Insert › Page Number…** puts in a page number field.
- Facts about the document come from [Document information fields](field-doc-info).
- **Insert › Cross-Reference…** refers to a bookmark ([Cross-references](cross-references)).

## Keeping them current

[Updating and locking fields](field-updating) explains F9, Ctrl+F9, Lock Field, and moving between fields.

## Seeing them

View › Show has **Show Power Fields**, which shows each field's code instead of its result (Alt+F9), and **Field Shading**, which tints fields so they stand out.
