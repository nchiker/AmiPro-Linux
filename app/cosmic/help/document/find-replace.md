Edit › Find… (Ctrl+F) opens the **find bar** over the pages, under the ruler. The document stays live while it is open. Edit › Replace… (Ctrl+H) opens it with the Replace row shown. A selected word fills the Find box.

## Using it

- **Enter** or **F3** goes to the next match; **Shift+Enter** or **Shift+F3** to the previous. The matches on the visible pages are highlighted and the bar counts them ("3 of 12").
- **Replace** replaces the current match and moves on. **Replace All** replaces every match in one step; Undo takes it all back.
- **Esc** closes the bar and leaves the last match selected.

## Options

The Options menu on the bar has Whole word only, Match case, Keep the found text's capitals, From the beginning, Include headers, footers, and frames, and Paragraph styles (to replace one style with another). It also has Attributes, to find or replace by character formatting.

Like Ami Pro, a replacement takes on the capitals of the text it replaces unless Match case is on. In the Find box, `?` stands for one character and `*` for one or more letters or digits. Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Enter type a tab and a return into a box.

The search covers the main text, frames, footnotes, headers, and footers, in that order.
