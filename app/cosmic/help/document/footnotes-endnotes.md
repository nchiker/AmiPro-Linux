A footnote is a numbered remark that appears at the foot of the page its mark is on. Endnotes are the same notes gathered after the text.

## Adding a footnote

Choose Insert › Footnote (Alt+Ctrl+F). A numbered mark goes in at the caret and the caret moves to the note's text at the foot of the page. Type the note there. The numbers follow the order of the marks, and renumber when you add or remove one.

## Footnote options

Tools › Footnote Options… opens the Footnotes panel in the rail. Each change shows at once and is one undo step.

- **Make endnotes** moves every footnote to the end of the document. This is one choice for the whole document: a document has footnotes or endnotes, not both.
- **Reset number on each page** starts the numbering again on each page.
- **Starting number** sets the first number.
- **Separator line** controls the rule between the text and the notes.
- **Continued messages** are the optional lines shown when a long footnote runs onto the next page.

A footnote that does not fit moves to the next page with its line of text, and a very long one can split across pages.

## Limits

A document with a footnote in its main text uses a slower full layout when you edit. In a master document the numbers carry on from one file to the next; see [Master documents](master-documents).
