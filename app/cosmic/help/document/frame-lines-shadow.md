Frame Properties › Lines and shadows decorates the selected frame. Each setting applies on the spot and is one undo step.

## Lines

- Tick the sides to draw: left, right, top, and bottom.
- **Line style (1–8)** picks the weight and kind of line by number.
- **Line position** decides where the line sits relative to the frame's edge: Middle, Inside, Outside, Close to inside, or Close to outside.
- **Line colour** is chosen from the colour swatches. The page shows a line's real width; the frame's text area is pulled in to match.

## Shadow

**Shadow** is None, Normal, Deep, or Custom. With a shadow on, **Shadow depth** and **Shadow falls** (Bottom right, Bottom left, Top left, Top right) say how far and which way, and **Shadow colour** picks its colour.

## Background and corners

**Background** colours the area inside the frame. **Rounded corners (%)** rounds the corners by a percentage of the frame's size. Rounded corners are drawn rounded on screen, in print, and in PDF.

## Notes

Lines for a whole page are set in [Page lines](page-lines), and those above or below paragraphs in the style's Lines section.
