**Edit › Go To…** (Ctrl+G) jumps to a page by number, to the first or last page, or to the next frame. The **Page X of Y** button in the status bar opens the same box.

## Other jumps

- **Previous Page** and **Next Page** put the caret at the top of that page's text. The arrows beside the page button in the status bar do the same.
- **Go To Header** and **Go To Footer** put the caret in the page's header or footer so you can type there. If the text no longer fits, the status bar warns you that it is hidden.
- **Go To Ruler** moves the keyboard into the [ruler](ruler-view) so the arrow keys adjust margins, indents, and tabs.
