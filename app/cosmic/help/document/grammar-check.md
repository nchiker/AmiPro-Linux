Grammar Check reads the text sentence by sentence and points out likely problems. It is a page of the Proofing panel.

## Running it

Choose Tools › Grammar Check…. Each problem selects the words concerned and shows an explanation with replacement choices. You can **Replace** (the sentence is then checked again), **Skip**, **Skip Rule** (ignore that kind of problem for the rest of this check), or go to the **Next Sentence**. The panel keeps a choice of rule set, which is saved in the document. With a selection, only the selection is checked.

The checker is built into the program and works offline. It knows English only, including the British, Australian, and Canadian forms; for other languages the panel says that Grammar Check is available for English documents only. When nothing is found it says "No problems were found."; at the end of a check it says "Grammar check complete."

## Readability statistics

Tools › Readability Statistics… shows counts of words, sentences, and syllables, averages, passive sentences, and the usual readability scores for the whole document or the selection. They are also shown at the end of a grammar check when that option is on.

## Underlines while typing

Grammar problems can be underlined in blue as you type. This is off by default. See [Spelling as you type](spell-as-you-type).
