A header repeats at the top of each page and a footer at the bottom. They sit inside the top and bottom margins and are formatted like any other text.

## Fixed headers and footers

Each page layout can have one fixed header and one fixed footer. Click in the top or bottom margin to type in it; headers and footers are edited in place on the page. [Go To](go-to) can put the caret in them from the keyboard. With a header or footer under the caret, Page Layout Properties opens at its section, where you set its margins, columns, tabs, and **Begin on second page**, which leaves it off page one. A header or footer made smaller than the margin shrinks its own margins to fit.

Because they belong to the layout, different pages can have different ones: use right and left pages for odd and even text (see [Page Layout Properties](page-layout-panel)), or [insert a page layout](insert-page-layout) part-way through the document.

## Floating headers and footers

Insert › Floating Header/Footer… puts a header or footer at the caret that applies from the next page on. The dialog offers:

- **Insert at the caret** with a choice of floating header or floating footer, and **Apply to** All pages, Right pages, or Left pages.
- **Remove the one at the caret**, which opens ready when a floating one is at the caret.

Insert puts the caret inside the new one so you can type at once.

To put the page number in, see [Page numbering](page-numbering).
