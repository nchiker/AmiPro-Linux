An indent moves a paragraph's lines in from the margins of the column it sits in. Format › Increase Indent (Ctrl+]) and Decrease Indent (Ctrl+[) change it in steps.

## Steps

Each press changes the paragraph's All indent by half an inch, for every paragraph the selection touches.

## The four values

For fine control use [Paragraph Properties](paragraph-properties) or drag the markers on the ruler:

- **All** moves every line in from the left.
- **First** is extra indent for the first line only; it adds to All.
- **Rest** is extra for every line after the first. A style can also be marked Hanging indent in Style Properties › Alignment and indents.
- **From right** moves the right edge in.

On the ruler, First is the upper triangle, Rest the lower one, All the bar between them, and the right indent a triangle at the right edge. Hover over a marker to see its name and place in the status bar, and drag it; the change applies when you release, and Esc during the drag cancels it.

A list item's bullet or number sits in the space made by First; see [Bullets and numbering](bullets-numbering).
