Line numbers appear in the left margin beside the lines of the text, on screen, in print, and in PDF. Format › Line Numbering… opens Page Layout Properties at its Line numbering section. The setting is for the whole document, not for one layout.

## Options

- **None**, **Number all lines**, or **Number text lines**. All lines counts blank lines and every kind of paragraph; text lines counts only body text.
- How often a number is shown: **Every line**, every other line, every fifth line, or every N lines, with N typed in place.
- **Reset each page** restarts at 1 on each page.
- **Based on**, for text lines, shows the style whose font the numbers use. It is Body Text.

Each change is one undo step.

## Tips

For the numbers to line up with the lines, use the same font and line spacing in every main-text style, with no space above or below. Draft view shows no line numbers. They are stored in Ami Pro documents.

Line numbering is a different thing from the numbers of a [list](bullets-numbering).
