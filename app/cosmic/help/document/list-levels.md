A numbered list can have items under items. Format › Bullets and Numbering › Demote (Alt+Right) pushes the caret's item one level deeper, and Promote (Alt+Left) brings it back out. The commands are described with the outline commands in [Promote, demote, and move headings](outline-promote-move); this topic covers what they do to a list.

## What happens to a numbered list

The first Demote on an item of a plain numbered list turns the whole run into **List 1** and the demoted item into **List 2**. Deeper levels are made as you need them: List 3, List 4, and so on, each a quarter inch further in. The numbering schemes by depth are 1. A. i. a. 1) a) i) (1) (a), and every level restarts its count after the level above.

Promote moves an item up one level. List 1 does not promote further.

The status bar names the style and level you landed on, or says the paragraph is already at the top or lowest level. Over a selection the change is one undo step.

## Customising

The list styles are ordinary styles. To change the look of one level, open it with [Modify Style](modify-style) or [Bullet and number style](bullet-number-style). To number headings by level, see [Outline numbering](outline-styles).
