Format › Styles › Modify Style… opens **Style Properties** in the rail for the paragraph at the caret. So does a right-click in text, the Styles panel's row menu, and Create Style's Modify button. Every paragraph in that style changes while you edit, so the page itself is the preview.

## How the panel works

- It follows the caret to another paragraph's style while open. The Style list at the top picks a different style, which stays until the caret moves into a paragraph of another style.
- Sections fold: **Font**, **Alignment and indents**, **Tabs**, **Spacing**, **Breaks and next style**, **Bullets and numbers**, **Lines**, and **Table format**, followed by a **Hyphenation** check box.
- A check box or list applies at once. A typed value applies on Enter or when you leave the box. A change is one undo step, and a value that does not read is refused in place with the reason.
- **Save as New Style** makes another style from the settings shown.

## What the sections hold

- **Font:** face, size, colour, Bold, Italic, Underline, Word underline, Double underline, Caps, and **1st line bold**. 1st line bold sets the first line of each paragraph in bold; checking it unchecks Bold and the reverse.
- **Alignment and indents:** alignment, All, First, Rest, From right, Both sides, Hanging indent.
- **Tabs:** the style's own stops; see [Tabs](tabs).
- **Spacing:** line spacing, space above and below, Add in (Always or When not at break), and text tightness.
- **Breaks:** page and column breaks before and after, Allow break within, Keep with previous and next paragraph, and the Next style that follows when you press Enter at the end.
- **Lines:** a rule above and below the paragraph, with style, spacing, length (Text, Margins, or Other), and colour.
- **Table format:** how numbers show in a table cell of this style; see [Tables](tables).

The Bullets and numbers section is covered in [Bullet and number style](bullet-number-style).
