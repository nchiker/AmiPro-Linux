Three features put information beside or behind the text: notes, bookmarks, and footnotes. This topic covers notes; the others have their own topics: [Footnotes and endnotes](footnotes-endnotes), [Bookmarks](bookmarks), and [Cross-references](cross-references).

## Notes

**Insert › Note** (Alt+Ctrl+M) adds a small coloured mark at the caret with a note attached. Notes are comments: they carry the author's initials and a time, show when View › Show › Notes is on, and print only if you ask for them in Print Options. Find and Word Count skip them.

When you export to DOCX, ODT, or RTF, a note becomes a comment.
