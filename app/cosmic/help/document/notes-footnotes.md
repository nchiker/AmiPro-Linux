These three put information beside or behind the text.

## Notes

**Insert › Note** (Alt+Ctrl+M) adds a small coloured mark at the caret with a note attached. Notes are comments: they carry the author's initials and a time, show when View › Show › Notes is on, and print only if you ask for them in Print Options. Find and Word Count skip them.

## Bookmarks

**Edit › Bookmarks…** (Ctrl+Shift+F5) opens the Bookmarks panel. Type a name (no spaces), and Add to mark the selection, a table cell, or a frame. Go To jumps back; Remove deletes the bookmark. View › Show › Bookmarks makes them visible. Cross references and some fields refer to bookmarks by name.

## Footnotes and endnotes

**Insert › Footnote** (Alt+Ctrl+F) numbers a reference mark at the caret and takes you to the footnote text at the foot of the page. **Tools › Footnote Options…** opens the Footnotes panel: make endnotes of every footnote, restart the numbers on each page, the starting number, the separator line, and the "continued" messages. Each change is live and one undo step.

**Insert › Cross-Reference…** inserts a field that shows the page or text of a bookmark.

All three are carried when you export to DOCX, ODT, or RTF; a note becomes a comment there.
