View › Outline holds the commands that decide how much of the outline shows. They work in Outline mode and in the Outline navigator; collapsed text is only hidden on screen, is kept per tab, and is never saved or printed differently.

- **Contract** (Alt+PageUp) hides the whole section under the heading the caret is in. Its mark becomes a darker plus.
- **Expand** (Alt+PageDown) shows that section down to body text.
- **Show 1 Level** to **Show 9 Levels** show headings down to that level and hide deeper headings and all body text. This clears any earlier contracting.
- **Show All Levels** shows everything. The current choice is checked in the menu.

The chevrons in the navigator contract and expand in step with these commands. The same buttons are on the Outline bar.
