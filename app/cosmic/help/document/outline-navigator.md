**View › Outline › Outline Navigator** opens the headings of the document as a tree in the rail. It works in the normal view and in Outline mode.

- The heading the caret is in is highlighted. Click a heading to go there.
- Drag a heading onto another to move its whole section before that heading; a line shows where it will land. Drop it below the tree to send it to the end. This is one undo step.
- The chevrons contract and expand a section, in step with Outline mode.
- After clicking in the tree, Up and Down move between headings, Left contracts, and Right expands.

If the list is empty it says "No headings": give a style an outline level in its properties (or use Promote) so its paragraphs are listed.
