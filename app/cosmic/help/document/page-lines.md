A page layout can draw a line around the text area of each page and a line between its columns. The settings are in Page Layout Properties › Lines (Format › Page Layout…).

## Around the page

Tick **All**, or any of **Left**, **Right**, **Top**, and **Bottom**. Once a side is on, three more controls appear:

- the **line style**, one of twelve (single lines of several weights, double and triple lines);
- the **position** of the line relative to the margin: Inside, Close to inside, Middle, Close to outside, or Outside;
- the **colour**.

## Between columns

**Line between columns** draws a rule in each gutter of a layout with more than one column. It has its own line style. The colour is shared with the page lines.

Lines belong to the layout, so every page that uses it shows them, and a page that switches to another layout switches its lines. They are drawn on screen, in print, and in PDF in the same places.

For lines on a single frame, see [Frame lines, shadow, and colour](frame-lines-shadow). A rule above or below a paragraph is a style setting: see [Modify Style](modify-style).
