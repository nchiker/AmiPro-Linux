Insert › Page Number… puts a page-number field at the caret. It is usually placed in a header or footer, where it shows each page's own number.

## The dialog

- **Style** is the look of the number: 1, 2, 3; I, II, III; i, ii, iii; A, B, C; or a, b, c.
- **Start on page** and **Start with number** make the numbering begin at a later page, or at a number other than 1, for instance after a title page.
- **Leading text** is words shown before the number, such as Page followed by a space. It is up to 30 characters.

A sample line tells you what the caret's page will show. Insert adds the field; if the caret is already in a page-number field, the button reads Change and edits that field instead.

## Afterwards

The field is an ordinary [field](fields), so it can be formatted, selected, and deleted like one. To have a different number style in a later part of a document, put a second field with its own style in a later [header or footer](headers-footers).
