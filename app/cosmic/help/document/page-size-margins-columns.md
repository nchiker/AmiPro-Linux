These settings are in Page Layout Properties (Format › Page Layout…), in the sections Page and Margins and columns.

## Page

- **Size** is a list of six common paper sizes and Custom. Width and Height show beside it; typing in either one makes the size Custom.
- **Portrait** and **Landscape** turn the page on its side.
- Lengths are in inches, shown with two decimals.

## Margins and columns

- **Left**, **Right**, **Top**, and **Bottom** margins. The top and bottom must leave some height for text.
- **Columns** is a list from 1 to 8. The columns are spread evenly across the text area.
- **Gutter** is the gap between columns; it shows only with two or more columns.
- **Column balance** evens out the columns on the last page of a stretch of columns; it is unavailable with one column.

Changing a margin makes columns whose gaps were alike even again; if the gaps differ, only the outer edge moves. Dragging column edges on the ruler gives uneven columns, and then the panel offers Even Columns to make them equal again.

## On the ruler

The lower half of the ruler shows the margins and columns of the box the caret is in. Drag an edge or gap to change it; a drag snaps to sixteenths of an inch and applies when released. Right-click the lower half for Columns 1–8, Even Columns, and Page Layout…. See [Rulers](rulers).

Frames and table cells have columns and margins of their own; see [Frame Properties](frame-properties).
