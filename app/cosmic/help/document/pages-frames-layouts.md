A document here is built the way Ami Pro builds it, which is not the way Word does. It is made of pages, page layouts, and frames.

## Pages and page layouts

Every page has exactly one **page layout**: its margins, columns, tab stops, header and footer, and page lines. The document's style sheet supplies the layouts, and [Insert Page Layout](insert-page-layout) starts a different one part-way through.

- [Page Layout Properties](page-layout-panel): Format › Page Layout… opens a layout's settings in the rail; right-clicking in a page's margin opens the same panel.
- [Size, margins, and columns](page-size-margins-columns), [page lines](page-lines), and [tab stops](tabs).
- [Headers and footers](headers-footers) and [page numbers](page-numbering).
- [Rulers](rulers) change tabs and indents from one paragraph on, without a new layout.
- [Page and column breaks](page-column-breaks) end a page or a column early; [line numbering](line-numbering) numbers the lines in the margin.

## Frames

A **frame** is a small document inside the document. It holds exactly one of: text, a table, or a picture (drawings, charts, and equations are pictures).

- [Creating frames](create-frame): Insert › Frame… or Draw Frame.
- [Frame Properties](frame-properties): size, position, and the rest.
- [Placement and wrap](frame-placement-wrap), [lines, shadow, and colour](frame-lines-shadow), and [columns and margins](frame-text-columns).
- [Layering and grouping](frame-layering-groups), and [pictures in frames](frame-pictures).

## Moving around

[Go To](go-to) (Ctrl+G) and the Go To Header, Footer, and Ruler commands jump to a page or part of one.
