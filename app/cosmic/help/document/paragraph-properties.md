Format › Paragraph… opens Paragraph Properties in the rail for the paragraph at the caret, or for all the paragraphs a selection touches. Changes show on the page as you make them, and each is one undo step.

## Sections

- **Indention:** All, First, Rest, and From right, in inches. First and Rest are added to All. See [Indents](indents).
- **Spacing:** line spacing Single, 1 1/2, Double, or Custom with a height typed in place. Space above and below are shown from the style.
- **Alignment:** Left, Center, Right, and Justify; see [Alignment](alignment).
- **Breaks:** page and column breaks before and after, and Allow break within, shown from the style.

Each section says whether its values are set for this paragraph or come from the style, and **Revert to style** puts the section back on the style's values.

## What is not editable here

Space above and below and the breaks are kept in the style, so the panel shows them and points you to [Modify Style](modify-style) to change them. Tabs have their own places: see [Tabs](tabs).

Values are typed in place and apply on Enter or when you leave the box. A value that does not read is refused with a reason and the old value stays.
