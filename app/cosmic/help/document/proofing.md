The proofing tools are in the Tools menu and share one Proofing panel in the rail. The document stays open and editable while the panel is shown.

## The tools

- [Spell check](spell-check) (F7) walks through unknown words and offers fixes.
- [Spelling as you type](spell-as-you-type) underlines unknown words as you write.
- [Thesaurus](thesaurus) (Shift+F7) offers synonyms for the word at the caret.
- [Grammar check and readability](grammar-check) finds common problems in English text and reports readability scores.
- [Document language](document-language) picks the dictionary and hyphenation rules.
- [Word count and statistics](word-count) (Ctrl+Shift+C) shows the size of the document.

When something fails, see [Proofing messages and what to do](proofing-messages).
