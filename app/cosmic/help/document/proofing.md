The proofing tools are in the Tools menu and share one panel in the rail.

## Spelling

**Spell Check…** (F7) goes through the document from the caret and shows each unknown word with suggestions. You can replace it, skip it, or add it to your dictionary. **Check Spelling as You Type** underlines unknown words while you write. The checker uses the Hunspell dictionaries installed on your system, plus a bundled American English one.

## Thesaurus

**Thesaurus…** (Shift+F7) lists synonyms for the word at the caret and replaces it with the one you choose.

## Grammar and readability

**Grammar Check…** looks for common problems in English. **Readability Statistics…** shows readability figures for the text.

## Language

**Language…** sets the document's language, which chooses the dictionary and the hyphenation rules. Hyphenation follows the language too.

## Word Count

**Word Count** (Ctrl+Shift+C) reports words, characters, and paragraphs.
