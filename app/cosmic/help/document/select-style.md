Format › Styles › Select a Style (Ctrl+Y) moves the keyboard to the style field in the action bar and opens its list, so you can apply a paragraph style without the mouse.

## Applying a style

1. Put the caret in a paragraph, or select several.
2. Press Ctrl+Y.
3. Choose the style from the open list; it applies to the paragraph or paragraphs.

The same list is available in other ways: click the style field in the action bar, click a name in the Styles panel in the rail, or right-click in text and use the style list that follows the first items of the menu.

## Reading the list

A style that is stored in the document, because you made or changed it, is marked with a bullet in front of its name. A name shown in the error colour is used by the text but defined nowhere; it is formatted as Body Text until you give it a definition. See [Style Management](style-management).

Applying a style replaces the paragraph's style but leaves its [direct formatting](clear-direct-formatting) in place.
