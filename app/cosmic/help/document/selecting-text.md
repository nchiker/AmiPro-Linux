**Edit › Select All** (Ctrl+A) selects everything in the text you are editing: the main text, or the frame, header, footer, or table cell that holds the caret.

Other ways to select: drag with the mouse, or hold Shift while moving the caret with the arrow keys. Commands such as Cut, Copy, Delete, style changes, and Find and Replace act on the selection.

Typing replaces a selection. Press an arrow key to drop it.
