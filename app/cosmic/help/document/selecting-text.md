**Edit › Select All** (Ctrl+A) selects everything in the text you are editing: the main text, or the frame, header, footer, or table cell that holds the caret.

Other ways to select: drag with the mouse, or hold Shift while moving the caret with the arrow keys. To select more than the window shows, keep the button held and move the pointer above or below the pages: they scroll, faster the farther you go, and the selection follows, from page to page. A drag stays in the text it started in: passing over a header, footer, or frame does not start a new selection there. Shift+click and Shift+PageUp or PageDown also scroll to keep the caret in view. Commands such as Cut, Copy, Delete, style changes, and Find and Replace act on the selection.

Typing replaces a selection. Press an arrow key to drop it.
