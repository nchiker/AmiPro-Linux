**View › Show** switches on aids that help while you edit. None of them print.

- **Tabs & Returns** draws a mark for each tab, space, and paragraph end. It also has a toolbar button.
- **Column Guides** draws a line between columns.
- **Margins in Colour** tints the area outside the margins.
- **Pictures**: when off, pictures are drawn as empty boxes, which scrolls faster in picture-heavy documents.
- **Table Row/Column Headings** labels tables with column letters and row numbers, so you can read cell addresses such as B3 for formulas.

The same choices, plus Power Fields, Field Shading, Notes, and Bookmarks, are in View › Show and in Preferences › View. Fields, notes, and bookmarks have their own topics: [Fields](fields), [Notes and footnotes](notes-footnotes).

The marks are kept for each tab separately.
