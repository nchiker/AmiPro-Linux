Format › Special Effects… opens a small dialog of check boxes for effects that Bold, Italic, and Underline do not cover. It is also in the right-click menu in text.

## The effects

- **Superscript** raises the text and shrinks it.
- **Subscript** lowers it and shrinks it.
- **Double underline** draws two lines under the text.
- **Strikethrough** draws a line through it.
- **Overstrike character** prints one character of your choosing over each character of the selection. Type the character in the box beside it; the box is checked when you type one.

Each box starts checked when the selected text already has the effect. OK changes only the boxes you changed, as one undo step, and leaves the rest alone.

## Notes

For a footnote number use [Insert › Footnote](footnotes-endnotes) rather than a superscript, because it numbers itself and keeps the note with its page.

These effects are direct formatting. [Normal](clear-direct-formatting) removes them.
