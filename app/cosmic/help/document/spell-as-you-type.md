With **Tools › Check Spelling as You Type** on, words that are not in the dictionary get a red wavy underline while you write. The underline is only on screen; it never prints.

## How it behaves

- The check waits a moment after you stop typing, then looks at the paragraphs whose text changed. It does not slow the keys down.
- A word repeated twice in a row is underlined too.
- The setting is saved with your preferences and is on by default. It applies to every document, not just the current one.
- Grammar problems can be underlined in blue as well; that is off by default (see [Grammar check](grammar-check)).

## Fixing an underlined word

Right-click it. The menu starts with suggestions: choose one to replace the word. Below them are Ignore All, Add to Dictionary, and Spell Check…, which opens the full check in the rail ([Spell check](spell-check)). On a repeated word the menu offers to delete the repeat.

A document in a language with no installed dictionary is not underlined. [Proofing messages and what to do](proofing-messages) says what to install.
