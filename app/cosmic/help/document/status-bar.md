**View › Status Bar** shows or hides the strip at the bottom of the window.

From the left it holds:

- The previous-page and next-page arrows, and the **Page X of Y** button, which opens Go To.
- The word count, and the count of the selection when there is one.
- A message area. Success messages fade; errors stay until something replaces them. It also tells you when text is hidden because its box is too small.
- The view mode and the zoom controls: zoom out, a slider, the percentage, and zoom in.

The style of the paragraph is not repeated here; use the style field in the toolbar or the Styles panel.
