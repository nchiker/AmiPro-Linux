Character formatting is in the Format menu, the action bar, and the right-click menu.

## Characters

- **Bold, Italic, Underline** (Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U) and **Word Underline** apply to the selection. With only a caret they set the formatting of the text you type next.
- **Font…** and **Special Effects…** choose face, size, colour, and effects such as superscript.
- Format › Caps has Upper Case, Lower Case, Initial Caps, and Small Caps.
- **Normal** (Ctrl+M) removes direct formatting and returns to the style.
- **Fast Format** (Ctrl+T) copies one place's formatting onto text you then select.

## Paragraphs

- **Paragraph…** opens the paragraph's settings in the rail: alignment, indents, spacing, tabs, and breaks.
- Align with Ctrl+L, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+R, and Ctrl+J. Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[ change the indent.
- **Bullets** (Ctrl+Shift+L) and **Numbering** (Ctrl+Shift+N) turn paragraphs into lists. Promote and Demote change a list item's level; Move Up and Move Down move it with its subordinates. Bullet and Number Style… picks the look.

## Marking text

Edit › Mark Text has Protected Text and No Hyphenation. Insert › Special Character… inserts symbols.

[Styles](styles) are the better place for settings that repeat.
