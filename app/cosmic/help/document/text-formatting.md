Text formatting is in the Format menu, the action bar, and the right-click menu. Each part has its own topic.

## Characters

- [Font, size, and colour](fonts-and-size): Format › Font….
- [Bold, italic, and underline](bold-italic-underline), with Word Underline.
- [Upper case, lower case, and small caps](capitalisation), and [special effects](special-effects) such as superscript.
- [Normal](clear-direct-formatting) (Ctrl+M) removes direct formatting; [Fast Format](fast-format) (Ctrl+T) copies it.
- [Special characters](special-characters), and [Protected Text and No Hyphenation](protected-text-hyphenation) under Edit › Mark Text.

## Paragraphs

- [Alignment](alignment): Ctrl+L, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+J.
- [Paragraph Properties](paragraph-properties) for indents, spacing, and breaks; [indents](indents) in steps; [tab stops](tabs).
- [Bullets and numbering](bullets-numbering), [their style](bullet-number-style), and [nested lists](list-levels).

[Styles](styles) are the better place for settings that repeat.
