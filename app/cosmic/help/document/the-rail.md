The **rail** is the column on the right of the window. It shows one panel at a time: Page Layout, Table, Fields, Print, Help, and so on. Most commands that need many settings open their panel here instead of a dialog, and the document stays visible and editable while you change them.

When nothing else is open, the rail shows the **Styles panel**, the list of paragraph styles in the document. Click a style to apply it. **View › Styles Panel** shows or hides the rail's default.

Press Esc, or the close button, to close the open panel and return to the Styles list. Opening another panel replaces the current one. The rail is not shown in Show Clean Screen.
