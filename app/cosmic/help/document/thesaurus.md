The thesaurus offers other words with the same meaning as the word at the caret. It is one page of the Proofing panel in the rail.

## Using it

1. Put the caret in a word, or click it.
2. Choose Tools › Thesaurus… (Shift+F7). The word is selected and the panel lists its meanings, each with its part of speech.
3. Pick a meaning to see its synonyms. Choose one and press **Replace**; the panel stays open so you can try another word.
4. **Look Up** searches for a synonym or any other word, and **Back** returns through the words you looked up.

The replacement follows the original: a word in capitals gets a synonym in capitals, and a verb in the past tense gets a synonym in the past tense, as far as the rules of the language allow. The right-click menu on any word also has a Synonyms submenu with the first meaning's choices.

## Where the words come from

The program reads MyThes thesaurus files installed on the system, such as the ones that ship with LibreOffice. If none exists for the document's language, the panel says so; see [Proofing messages and what to do](proofing-messages). The language is set under [Document language](document-language).
