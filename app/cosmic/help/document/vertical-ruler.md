**View › Vertical Ruler** shows a ruler down the left side of the pages. It scrolls with them and follows the zoom, and marks each page's margins in inches: the top margin (where the header sits) and the bottom margin (where the footer sits) are tinted, and the text area between them is white. When the caret is in a frame or a table, a blue bar beside the ruler shows how far down the page it reaches.

Drag the dark bar at the end of the top margin, or at the start of the bottom margin, to change that margin. The position snaps to a sixteenth of an inch, the status bar shows the margin while you drag, and a guide line runs across the page. Press Esc to let go without a change. The change is one step you can undo, and it applies to the same pages as Page Layout Properties does: all pages, or only the right or left pages when the layout has them.

The vertical ruler is off at first. It is a setting of the window, saved with your preferences; Preferences › View has the same switch. In Outline mode, which has no pages, it is hidden.
