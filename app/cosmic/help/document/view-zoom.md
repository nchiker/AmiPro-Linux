The View menu controls how the pages are shown, not how they print.

## Zoom

Zoom In and Zoom Out (Ctrl++ and Ctrl+-), Actual Size (Ctrl+0), Full Page (Ctrl+D), Enlarged (200%), Custom Zoom, and Fit Page Width.

## Page arrangement

**Continuous Pages** stacks the pages in one column. **Facing Pages** shows two pages side by side. **Draft Mode** (Ctrl+Shift+D) shows the text in one plain column without page layout, which is quicker to scroll and edit.

## Show

View › Show turns on marks that help while editing and never print: tabs and returns, column guides, margins in colour, pictures (off shows empty boxes), table addresses such as B3, power fields, field shading, notes, and bookmarks.

The Ruler, Status Bar, Toolbar, and Styles Panel items show and hide those parts of the window.
