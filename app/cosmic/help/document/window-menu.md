Every open document has its own tab. The **Window** menu moves between them.

- **Next Document** (Ctrl+PageDown) and **Previous Document** (Ctrl+Shift+Tab) step through the tabs.
- The names of the open documents are listed below; choose one to switch to it.

Ctrl+Tab is taken by the desktop before the program sees it, so it is not available.

Each tab keeps its own caret, selection, scroll position, zoom, view mode, and undo history. A dot or mark on a tab means unsaved changes.
