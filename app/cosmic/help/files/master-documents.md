A **master document** ties several documents together as one work, such as the chapters of a book. **File › Master Document…** opens the panel in the rail: add files in order, remove them, and set the output files for the table of contents and index.

When you build the contents, print, or update the set, the program goes through each member in turn and carries the numbers on: the page numbers, footnote numbers, and numbered styles continue from one file into the next. Each member is saved with the numbers it received. A member opened alone shows the numbers it had when last worked.

The table of contents and index cover the whole set and are made as their own documents. Printing the master prints the contents, the members in order, then the index.

If a member cannot be opened the run stops and says which. Using the command on a member rather than a master asks whether to treat it as a single document again.
