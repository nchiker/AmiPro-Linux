Merging fills a form document with records from a data file.

## Steps

1. Choose or create the **data file**. It can be an Ami Pro document, a table, or a CSV, TSV, DIF, fixed-width, dBASE, XLSX, or ODS file.
2. Put the cursor where a value goes and choose **Insert › Merge Field…**; pick a field name. It shows as a name between angle brackets.
3. Open **File › Merge…**. The panel in the rail offers merging to the printer, to a saved document, or to labels, with optional conditions that choose which records are used.

The document remembers its data file, so you can merge again later.

## Labels

Merge **as labels** sets the number across and down and prints each record on a label.

## Envelopes

**File › Print Envelope…** opens the Envelope panel: choose an envelope size, a return address, and the address (the selection, or the first address in the document).
