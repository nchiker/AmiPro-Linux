**File › Print…** (Ctrl+P) opens the Print panel in the rail: copies, the page range, even or odd pages, reverse order, collate, and options such as crop marks, printing without pictures, and printing notes. **Print Current Page…** prints just the page the caret is on.

The program draws pages for the printer the same way it draws them on screen, so what you see is what is sent. The printer itself is chosen in the desktop's print dialog, which opens when you confirm. Page ranges are decided by the program; copies, collate, and reverse are applied by the desktop.

## PDF

**File › Export › Export PDF…** (Ctrl+Shift+E) writes a PDF with the fonts embedded and the text selectable. PDF/A can be chosen. The PDF is made from the same layout as the page view.

Printing needs a printer or print-to-file option on your desktop, and has not yet been checked on every setup.
