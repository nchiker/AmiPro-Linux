Everything here is in the Chart panel and applies at once; each change is one undo step.

## Options

- **Legend**: a key to the series names, to the right of the plot.
- **Grid**: dashed lines behind the chart at the axis values.
- **3D**: a depth effect, with **Depth** from 1 to 100 as a number box and slider. Available for columns, bars, areas and pies.
- **Perspective**: strengthens the 3D effect. It is offered only with 3D on, and not for pies.

## Colour set

Sixteen swatches, one for each series (or category, for pies). Select one to change its colour with the colour button below it. Reordering the swatches by dragging is not built.

## Axis

Pies have no axis. For other charts:

- **Automatic** picks the numbers from the data and shows them greyed.
- Untick it to type **Inc** (the step between marks), **Min** and **Max**. Inc must be above 0, Max above Min, and there can be at most 100 steps. A wrong value is explained in place and not applied.
