At the bottom of the Chart panel:

- **Copy Data as CSV** puts the chart's data on the clipboard as comma-separated text, ready to paste into a spreadsheet.
- **Save Chart as Image…** opens the desktop's save dialog; choose PNG or SVG. The picture is drawn by the same renderer as Save Drawing As.
- **Chart from Table** builds a chart from the table at the caret ([Chart from Table](chart-from-table)).
- **Drawing…** switches to Draw mode on the chart ([How charts are stored](chart-storage)).

In exported documents (DOCX, ODT, RTF, PDF) a chart is a picture; see [Import and export](import-export).
