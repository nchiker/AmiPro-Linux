Grouping ties shapes together so they move as one, and the stacking commands choose which shape covers which.

## Group and ungroup

Select several objects and choose **Draw › Group**. They then move, size, turn and flip as one. **Ungroup** separates a selected group again. Groups can contain groups.

## Stacking order

Objects drawn later cover earlier ones. Change that with:

- **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back**: all the way;
- **Bring Forward** and **Send Backward**: one step.

Ctrl-click with the arrow tool selects the next object below at the pointer, which helps to reach one that is covered.
