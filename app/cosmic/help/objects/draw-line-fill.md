The look of a drawn object is its line (the pen) and its fill.

- **Draw › Line and Fill…** shows the Line and Fill sections of [Drawing Properties](drawing-properties) beside the page.
- **Draw › Extract Line & Fill** remembers the look of the selected object.
- **Draw › Apply Line & Fill** puts the remembered look on the selected objects. Use this to make several shapes match.

Lines take colours, a width, a dash style and an ending at either end. Closed shapes also take a fill pattern and a background. A line or fill set while nothing is selected becomes the look of objects drawn afterwards.
