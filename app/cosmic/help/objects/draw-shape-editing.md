Rotate, Flip and Duplicate change the selected shapes without redrawing them.

## Rotating

- **Draw › Rotate Left 90°** and **Rotate Right 90°** turn the selection by a quarter turn.
- **Draw › Rotate by Step** turns by the angle set under *Rotate by* in the Position and size section of [Drawing Properties](drawing-properties), clockwise or not as that section says.

## Flipping

**Draw › Flip › Horizontal** and **Vertical** mirror the selection across its own middle.

## Resizing and reshaping

Drag a selected object's handles to resize it. Dragging a handle past the opposite side flips the object. Double-click a line, polyline or polygon to edit its points.

## Duplicating

**Draw › Duplicate** makes a copy of the selection beside the original.

Text objects can be turned like any other object, and turned text is drawn on screen and in the PDF.
