**Drawing Properties** is the rail panel that appears in Draw mode. It follows the selection: with nothing selected it sets the look of objects you will draw next. Each box applies when you press Enter or leave it, and each change is one undo step.

## Sections

- **Line**: width (from half a point up to about 12 points), dash style (solid, dashes, dots, dash dot, dash dot dot), colour, and the **Start** and **End** endings: none, arrow, notched arrow, open arrow, tail, notched tail, open tail, bar, square or dot.
- **Fill**: pattern (none, solid, four dot densities, horizontal, vertical, diagonal and cross hatches), the pattern's colour, and its background colour.
- **Position and size**: distance from the left and from the top, width and height, with **Keep proportions**; **Rotate by** an angle with a Clockwise choice; **Align** and **Distribute** buttons.
- **Text**: face, size, colour, bold, italic, underline, for text objects.
- **Grid**: show the grid, snap to it, its spacing, and whether it is drawn as dots or dotted lines.

Colours use the same colour picker as the rest of the program; see [Text formatting](text-formatting).

The Draw menu has the same commands for the selection, and the right-click menu in Draw mode offers them too.
