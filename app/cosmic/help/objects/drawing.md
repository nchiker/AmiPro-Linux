**Insert › Drawing…** makes a frame at the caret, or uses the selected empty frame, and turns on **Draw mode** in place. Double-click an existing drawing to edit it.

## In Draw mode

- A draw bar replaces the toolbar, with tools for lines, polylines, polygons, rectangles, rounded rectangles, ellipses, arcs, and text.
- The **Draw menu** is then enabled. The rail shows the properties of the selected shape: line, fill, and position.
- Shapes can be rotated, flipped, grouped, and ordered front to back. Align and Distribute line up several shapes; Snap and Show Grid help placement.
- **Extract Line & Fill** copies one shape's look and **Apply Line & Fill** puts it on others.
- **Import Drawing…** adds a drawing from a file; **Save Drawing As…** writes the drawing out.

## Leaving

Press Esc, choose **Done Drawing**, or click outside the drawing. Ami Pro's own drawings (`.sdw`) are read and written without changing what you did not touch.
