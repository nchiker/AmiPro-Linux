**Tools › Equations…** opens the Equation panel in the rail and makes an equation in a picture frame. The panel holds a palette of templates (fractions, roots, sums, integrals, and so on); the source text is typed in a card on the page, and the drawn equation updates as you type.

The source is Ami Pro's dialect of TeX, so equations from Ami Pro documents read and write unchanged. Control words that are not known are reported as errors rather than dropped.

**Tools › Paste LaTeX Equation** takes LaTeX from the clipboard (`$...$`, `\[...\]`, or an equation environment) and turns it into an equation.

The layout follows TeX's rules, fitted to Ami Pro's sizes, so it differs slightly from Ami Pro's drawing. About 330 of Ami Pro's 887 symbol names are known. In exported documents an equation appears as a picture.
