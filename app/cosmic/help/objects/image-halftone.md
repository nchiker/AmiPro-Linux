The **Halftone** list in the Image section of Frame Properties records how Ami Pro should turn a bitmap into printer dots. This program stores the choice with the picture and writes it into Ami Pro files; it does not use it itself.

## The five choices

- **Fastest**: the default; Ami Pro prints it quickest.
- **Best picture quality**: the most careful rendering in Ami Pro.
- **Automatically selected**: a balance for pictures that have been scaled.
- **Posterize**: in Ami Pro, a stark two-tone rendering with no grey shades.
- **Printer driver**: leaves the dots to the printer driver.

## What you will see here

The page, PDF output and desktop printing all draw the picture the same way whichever choice is set. The setting matters only when the document is later opened and printed in Ami Pro. See [Printing and PDF](printing-pdf).
