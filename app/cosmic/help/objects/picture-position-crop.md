A picture larger than its frame is clipped by the frame. Moving the picture inside the frame chooses which part shows, which is how a picture is cropped here. Nothing is cut away; moving it back reveals it again.

## With the mouse and keys

Double-click a picture frame, or press Enter on a selected one. The handles go grey and the status bar tells you that dragging or the arrow keys move the picture.

- Drag to move the picture inside the frame.
- Arrow keys move it by 0.05 inch; with Ctrl, 0.5 inch. A run of presses is one undo step.
- Esc, or a click elsewhere, returns to normal frame editing.

Holding Shift while dragging a corner handle sizes the frame and the picture together.

## By typing

In the Picture section of Frame Properties, **Moved right (in)** and **Moved down (in)** give the same offset as numbers. **Reset** puts the picture back at the corner of the frame with Fit in frame and the aspect ratio kept.

To crop to a particular size, shrink the frame (Frame Properties › Size and position), then move the picture inside it.

Exports that cannot clip a picture inside a larger frame may show the whole picture; see [Compatibility](compatibility).
