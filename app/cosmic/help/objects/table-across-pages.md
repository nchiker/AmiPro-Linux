A table that does not fit the rest of its column continues on the next page or column.

- A row moves on whole when its text does not fit. Each part starts with the table's top inset.
- With **Rows span pages** (Table panel) a row can split between two lines instead, and each cell breaks at its own line.
- **Heading rows** are drawn again at the top of every later part, and the caret can go into the repeats, which edit the real heading row.
- **Insert › Page Break** with the caret in a cell makes that row begin a new page; choosing it again in that row removes the break.
- A row taller than a page is cut to the page unless Rows span pages is on.
- Without **Automatic** row height, rows keep their size and their text is cut off.
- A table inside a frame never continues; it shows the rows the frame can hold and hides the rest.

Cell text growing makes the table paginate again as you type, as ordinary text does.
