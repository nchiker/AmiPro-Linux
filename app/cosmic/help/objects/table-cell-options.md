Headings, leaders and cell protection are options you set on cells of a table.

## Headings

**Headings** (Table menu, a checked item) marks the selected rows as heading rows. They stay at the top of the table and repeat when the table continues on a following page ([Tables across pages](table-across-pages)). The item is available for a table that sits in the page text. Sorting leaves heading rows in place.

## Leaders

A leader fills the empty space of a cell with a row of dots, dashes, or a line, as in a contents list. Choose **Table › Leaders** and one of None, Dots, Dashes, or Line. The check shows the current choice. How the leader is drawn follows the text's alignment.

## Protect Cells

**Protect Cells** (a checked item) locks the selected cells against typing. It takes effect when the table's *Honor protection* option is on in the Table panel; the caret then skips protected cells and Tab passes over them.

Protection and leaders are stored in Ami Pro's own terms and survive saving as an Ami Pro document.
