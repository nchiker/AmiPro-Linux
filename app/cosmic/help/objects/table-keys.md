These keys move the caret, add rows and select cells while you work inside a table.

- **Tab** moves to the next cell, **Shift+Tab** to the previous one. Hidden cells (joined away) and, when *Honor protection* is on, protected cells are skipped.
- **Tab in the last cell adds a new row** and moves into it. Shift+Tab in the first cell does nothing.
- **Enter** starts a new paragraph inside the cell; it does not move to another cell. The cell grows if its row is automatic height.
- **Ctrl+arrow keys** move a cell at a time; with Shift they extend a cell selection.
- **Alt+Up** and **Alt+Down** move the caret's row, or the selected rows, up or down.
- **Esc** steps out of the cell's text.
- A typed tab character is not inserted into a cell; Tab always moves.

In a cell that holds a formula, typing is refused; see [Formulas and Quick Add](table-formulas). The shortcuts of the whole program are in [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts).
