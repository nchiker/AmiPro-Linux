Table styles are ready-made sets of lines and shading you can apply to a whole table.

## Table styles

Table › Table Style gives the whole table a ready-made look in one undo step: **Grid** (lines everywhere), **Outline** (a line round the table), **Header Row** (the top row ruled and marked as a heading row), **Banded Rows** (alternate rows shaded), and **Plain** (no lines). The same choices are a row of previews in the panel's Table section. A style changes lines and shading only; text and sizes stay.

## Lines and fill, cell by cell

In the panel's *Cells* section pick the selected cells' sides: All, Outline, or each side, each with on, off, or mixed. Choose a line style from the list and a colour, or *Remove lines* to clear every side. A line thicker than the gap beside it widens the gap. *Fill* colours the cell background through the colour panel.

Other table options in the *Table* section: a line around the whole table, centring it on the page, honouring protected cells, rows spanning pages, and disabling mouse resizing.

## Limits

Ami Pro stores table lines in black only. A different line colour is kept when you save in the program's own format, but saving as an Ami Pro document writes black and lists "table line colours" in the loss check.
