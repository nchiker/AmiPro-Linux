Cell commands act on a selected rectangle of whole cells.

## Ways to select

- Drag from one cell into another.
- Shift+click a second cell, or hold Shift+Ctrl and use the arrow keys.
- Table › Select › Column, Row, or Entire Table.
- A press on the strip above a column or left of a row selects it.

Selected cells are drawn whole, in the accent colour. The table panel then shows the selection's range, such as B2:C3.

## What you can do with them

- **Delete** empties the cells but keeps rows and columns.
- **Copy** and **Cut** take the cells (and a tabbed text version). **Paste** fills cells from the caret's cell; a block copied to a bigger selection repeats. Tabbed text pasted into a table fills its cells.
- Drag a selected row, column, or block to move it; hold Ctrl to copy.
- Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the caret's row, or the selected rows.

Select Column needs a table with at least two rows. Commands that cannot act on the selection are greyed.
