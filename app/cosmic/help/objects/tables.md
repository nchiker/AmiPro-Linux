A table is a grid of cells that hold text, numbers, or formulas. In this program a table lives in a frame or sits in the text at the caret.

## Where things are

- Insert › Table… makes one: [Inserting a table](table-insert).
- The **Table** menu appears while the caret is in a table. It works on that table. Its right-click version has the row, column, and cell items.
- **Table Properties…** opens the Table panel in the rail, with sections for the table, its rows and columns, its cells, and its formula. Every change there is one undo step, and the panel follows the caret and any cell selection. A value the selected cells disagree on shows as "Mixed".
- Dragging a grid line resizes the column or row beside it; the ruler shows the columns too and its markers drag the same way.
