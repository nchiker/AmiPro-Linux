These are the messages the program shows while you work with fields, and what each one asks of you.

- **Field inserted.** The field is in the text. Nothing to do.
- **Field changed: Update to show its new result.** You edited a code; press F9 to work it out.
- **The field cannot go there.** The program could not put the field at the caret, for example because the caret is not in ordinary text. Move the caret into text and try again.
- **Only power fields lock.** Dates and document-information fields cannot be locked; they follow the document.
- **The field is locked: updates leave it.** Lock Field is checked. Choose it again to allow updates.
- **There is no field here to update. / The document has no fields.** The caret is not in a field, or there is none to refresh.
- **The fields were not updated.** You cancelled a prompt from Query$ or Decide.
- **Put the caret in a field to change it here.** The This field section only works on a field at the caret.
- **Power fields show their codes: edit them, then Update or Show Power Fields.** Shown when you turn on code view; edit a code, then update or switch back.
