**Tools › Preferences…** (Ctrl+,) opens a dialog with four pages. The settings belong to you, not to a document, and are stored in your COSMIC configuration.

- [General](preferences-general): undo levels, custom zoom, recent files, the default style sheet, backup, updates, and recovery copies.
- [Folders](preferences-folders): where Open and Save start, and where style sheets are searched.
- [View](preferences-view): which window parts and marks are shown.
- Toolbar: show or hide the bar, with a button for [Customize Toolbar](customize-toolbar).

Changes apply as soon as you make them. **Close** dismisses the dialog.
