**Tools › Preferences…** (Ctrl+,) opens a dialog with three pages, each in titled sections. The settings belong to you, not to a document, and are stored in your COSMIC configuration.

- [General](preferences-general): new documents (the default style sheet, the format to save in), saving (backup, recovery copies), editing (undo levels, recent files), and updates.
- [Folders](preferences-folders): where Open and Save start, and where style sheets are searched.
- [View](preferences-view): which window parts are shown (with [Customize Toolbar](customize-toolbar) beside the toolbar's switch), the custom zoom, and which marks the page shows.

Changes apply as soon as you make them. **Close** dismisses the dialog.
