These are the messages and disabled states you meet while sorting, and what each one asks of you.

- **Select text, or put the caret in a table.** Nothing is selected that could be sorted.
- **Select two or more paragraphs to sort. / Select two or more rows, or put the caret in the table to sort it all.** One item has no order. Extend the selection.
- **The table has fewer than two rows to sort.** Add rows first.
- **Level 1 needs a field number from 1 to 99.** Type the field to sort by.
- **The record size must be from 1 to 99.** Paragraphs in record needs a number in that range.
- **The field delimiter is one character.** Type a single character, or choose Tab.
- **Already in that order.** The rows are already sorted by that key; nothing changed.
- **A cell in the rows holds a formula, so they cannot be sorted.** Remove the formula (Table Properties › Formula › Remove formula) or sort other rows.
- **A table with footnotes cannot be sorted.** Move the footnotes out first.
- A question with **Continue** and **Cancel** appears when the range contains a page or column break, or connected cells. Continue sorts across the break or disconnects the cells.
