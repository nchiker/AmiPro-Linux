**Help › About AmiPro-Linux** shows the program's name, version, short description, and licence. Quote the version when you report a problem.

AmiPro-Linux is an independent program that reads and writes Ami Pro 3.1 documents. It is not made or supported by the owners of Ami Pro. For what it can and cannot do with your old files, see [Compatibility and fidelity](compatibility) and [Known limitations](known-limitations). Help › Check for Updates… looks for a newer version; see [Updates](updates).
