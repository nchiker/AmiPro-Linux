AmiPro-Linux is a page-based word processor for the COSMIC desktop. It opens Ami Pro documents (`.SAM`) and style sheets (`.STY`), and it also reads DOCX, ODT, RTF, Markdown, and plain text.

## The window

- The **menu bar** and the **action bar** (the toolbar) are at the top. Tools › Customize Toolbar changes the buttons.
- Each open document has a **tab**. Window › Next Document (Ctrl+PageDown) and Previous Document (Ctrl+Shift+Tab) move between them.
- The **ruler** shows the margins, columns, indents, and tabs of the paragraph the caret is in.
- The **page workspace** shows the pages as they will print. Text, frames, tables, and pictures are all edited right on the page.
- The **rail** on the right shows one panel at a time. By default it lists the styles; most commands that need many settings open their panel there instead of a dialog box. Press Esc to close a panel.
- The **status bar** at the bottom shows messages, the page, and the zoom.

## First steps

1. Press Ctrl+N for a new document, or File › New… to start from a style sheet. Ctrl+O opens a file.
2. Type. Choose a paragraph style from the style list in the action bar or the Styles panel.
3. Save with Ctrl+S. See [Saving and Ami Pro files](saving-files) before writing over an old document.
4. Ctrl+Z undoes. A run of typing undoes as one step.

Tools › Preferences (Ctrl+,) holds the settings. View › Show Clean Screen (F11) hides the chrome. Press F1 anywhere for the Help that fits what you are doing; see [Using Help](using-help).
